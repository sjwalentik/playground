Husky World - Pack Edition

A 3D interactive husky world where 3 huskies act as a pack!

## Features

### Pack Behaviors
- **Zoomies Mode** - All dogs run together at high speed
- **Pack Following** - Dogs follow you in formation
- **Pack Huddle** - Dogs circle up together periodically  
- **Smart AI** - More realistic pack dynamics

### Three Unique Huskies
- **Female Husky** (dark gray) - High-pitched barks
- **Male Husky** (brown/tan) - Low-pitched barks
- **Playful Pup** (light gray) - Mid-range barks

## Controls
- **WASD** - Move your character
- **Mouse** - Look around
- **Click** - Pet the dogs
- **Esc** - Release mouse

## How It Works
The huskies will wander, play with each other, have zoomies together, and follow you as a pack. They have individual personalities but act as a coordinated group!

## Technical Details
- Built with Three.js
- Browser-based audio synthesis for barks
- Real-time tail wag and leg animations
- Pack coordination algorithms
